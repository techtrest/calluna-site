Just your list.

A calm shared shopping list for two. No ads, no flyers, no tracking.

How it works

  • Know the moment they think of something.

    Add milk on your phone; it's on theirs a second later.

  • Add it in a second.

    Type it or say it, and Calluna puts it in the right category.

  • Know what to get where.

    Give items a shop, and see only what you need there.

Why Calluna

Calluna was made by a German developer, married and living in Poland. Every shopping list app the two of them tried had turned into something else: deals, flyers, recipes, ads inside the list itself. So he built the one they wanted: just the two of them and the things they need. The free version has no ads, and never will.

Invited by your partner?

Install Calluna from Google Play, then open the invite link they sent you, or enter their code. You'll be on the same list straight away.

Privacy, plainly

  • Your lists are stored on a Hetzner server in Germany that we run ourselves.
  • No ads, no analytics, no tracking tools in the app.
  • Export or delete everything yourself, any time, in the app.
  • Honest note: your lists are encrypted on the way to the server, but not end-to-end.
