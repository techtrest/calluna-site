Just your list.

A shopping list you share with one other person. No ads, no flyers, no tracking.

How it works

  • Both phones, one list.

    Add milk on your phone, and it's on theirs a second later. If they add a few things on the way home, you get one notification, not five.

  • Add it in a second.

    Type a few letters or just say it. Calluna finds the item and puts it in the right category.

  • Know what to get where.

    Give each item a shop. In the shop, tap it, and you see only what you need there.

Why Calluna

My wife and I share one shopping list. Every app we tried for it slowly turned into something else: deals, flyers, recipes, ads right inside the list.

So I built the one we actually wanted. Just the two of us and the things we need.

I'm Oliver, a German developer living in Poland. I build Calluna in the evenings, and we use it for our weekly shop. If something annoys us, it gets fixed.

Calluna has no ads, and it never will.

Got an invite?

Open the link they sent you to install Calluna from Google Play, or enter their code. You're on the same list straight away.

Privacy, plainly

  • Your lists are stored on a server in Germany, rented from Hetzner and run by me.
  • No ads, no analytics, no tracking tools in the app.
  • You can export or delete everything yourself, any time, in the app.
  • Honest note: your lists are encrypted on the way to the server, but not end-to-end. Technically I could read them. I don't.
